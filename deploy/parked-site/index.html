What RoomRally did

RoomRally was a party game engine for in-person groups — classrooms, youth groups, living rooms, team offsites. A host projected the game on a big screen, players joined from their phones with a 4-letter room code (no accounts, no downloads), and everyone played together in the same room.

Comedy Clash

Write funny answers to prompts. The room votes for the best.

A-List

Name as many as you can in a category before time runs out.

Think Fast

A multiple-choice trivia blitz that tests your reflexes.

Bandwagon

Live polls where the room piles onto the crowd favorite.

Hosts could also bring their own questions, prompts, and polls — saved as templates so the next game night launched in seconds.
